## Question

Which of three design directions should the Meeting UI (Meeting dropdown, Start meeting, Meeting settings, the live
meeting view) take? The spec requires three directions for the user to pick before it is built.

Resolve by making three cheap, rough artifacts and letting the user choose. The user makes the selection, not the agent.
Link the artifacts from this ticket. Cover at least: the dropdown entry points, the settings screen (limits, agenda
toggles / reorder / custom, attendees and who may call, outputs), and what a member's row shows while it finishes its
step and joins.
